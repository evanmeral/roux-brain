## 2 · Shoot list, priority order

### 1 — Turkey Fry Demo (30 QT Turkey Fryer, 6" Banjo Burner — frying mode)
**Job:** Thanksgiving prospecting + turkey bundle. Must be live by **Oct 1**. Remake of `Video_Jay 30qt Demo`.
**On camera: Stephen.** **Hook:** *"Oil to 350°. Clock's running."* **Shots:** clock + thermometer at
ignition → hold at 350° → bird lowered on the Single Upright rack → drain valve, lid → payoff: bird out,
carved, family table. **On-screen claim:** **350° in under 10 minutes** (frying mode — this is not the
boil claim). **Formats:** all four Meta cutdowns + 15/30/45s + stills of the carved bird and rig.

### 2 — 60 QT Shrimp Boil Demo (Single Jet Burner — boiling mode)
**Job:** proves the "boils in under 7 minutes" claim — the same claim as the 120 QT, now demonstrated on
the pot actually being pushed. Retargeting ("is the 7-minute thing real") + cold prospecting.
**On camera: Stephen** (hands) + the clock. **Hook:** *"Everybody thinks the 7-minute thing is marketing.
Here's the clock."* **Shots:** one continuous locked-off take — cold water at the fill line, thermometer
in the water, big digital timer, ignition to rolling boil, no cut that skips time → second vertical
camera, same take → flame through the Tunnel Tubes under the pot (**this is also where we grab the owed
tunnel-tube still**) → load a basket of shrimp, corn and potatoes, recovery run back to a boil → steam,
basket lift, pour. **On-screen:** the clock's actual reading, "Unedited. Real time," and the
ambient-conditions qualifier. **Formats:** all four Meta cutdowns + 15/30s speed-ramped + the full
real-time master + the tunnel-tube still.

### 3 — 18 QT Fry-It-All Demo
**Job:** refreshes `18qt-TOF-Prospecting` once its freeze ends; remakes the volume-winning
`Video_Jay 18qt Demo`. **On camera:** Jay. **Hook:** *"350° in under 5 minutes. Clock's running."*
**Shots:** thermometer climb and hold at 350° → menu in the customer's own words (steak fingers, fries,
okra, plus fish, wings, hushpuppies, beignets, soft-shell crab) → each food out and draining. **Claim:**
350° in under 5 minutes — this pot, the 4-Way and the 40 QT only. **Watch:** no crawfish. **Formats:** all
four Meta cutdowns + 15/30s + plated food stills.

### 4 — Tailgate Video (18 QT Tailgate Bundle) — confirmed staged at the shop
**Job:** supports the approved tailgate kit ($465: fryer, leg extensions, 12" thermometer, wind shield,
skimmer), live Sept 25. **On camera: Jay + Stephen.** **Hook:** oil bubbling on the 18 QT, thermometer at 350°, basket coming up —
*"Before kickoff."* **Shots:** 18 QT on leg extensions frying fish/wings/fries/hushpuppies and draining →
the full kit laid out → a tight staged tailgate spread (table, cooler, team colors) — full crowd/game
atmosphere not needed. **Watch:** no crawfish near the fryer, no team or school logos, team colors only.
**Formats:** all four Meta cutdowns + 15/30s + kit and food stills for the bundle page.

### 5 — "Buy it once": the price answer
**Job:** the #1 objection, "that's way too expensive," for retargeting. Argues quality, not price — 80%
of surveyed buyers chose on quality, 10% on price (reviews.io, n=20). **On camera:** Jay or Evan, with the
**60 QT Performance** in hand throughout — a couple of other pots can sit out on set for background
variety, but the 60 QT is what gets handled and demonstrated.
**Hook:** *"You can buy a cheap pot twice. Or buy this one once."* **Beats:** (1) costs less to own — an
ice-chest comparison with no brand named, then calipers on the 60 QT's 4mm wall, a weld bead close-up,
"with good care, these last generations"; (2) costs less to run — "up to 75% less propane," savings
calculator on screen; (3) the Tunnel Tubes as the reason for both. **Watch:** no specific lifespan claim,
no other brand or logo in frame, warranty stays out of the video. **Formats:** 30/60s, all four Meta
cutdowns.

### 6 — Shop Interviews + 12-Man Team raw coverage
**Job:** trust content now, plus raw material for a future standalone 12-man team brand film — no longer
NOLA-exclusive. **On camera:** welders and staff who are comfortable being filmed, **answering questions
while actively working** — welding, packing, or handling a pot — not a sit-down talking head. **Proposed
hook:** *"Meet the hands that build it."* (Evan: swap this if you want something else — low-stakes copy.)
**Content:** a couple of quick, in-motion Q&As (names on camera), a few candid portraits mid-task. **Check
first:** the Aug 18 Founders Spotlight already covers Jay and Robert — this is the crew, so it shouldn't
overlap. **Future film directions to pitch, once there's a dedicated day:**
1. **"Meet the crew"** — short vignettes on 3–4 people (name, role, one line on their craft).
2. **"Built by hand"** — hands-only sequence, welding to a customer's driveway, ends on the full team.
   Uses *"Built by hand by our 12-man team here in South Louisiana."* Approved: "Built in Louisiana,"
   "Hand-welded in Louisiana." ⛔ Never "Made in USA."
3. **"From tube to table"** — one pot's build, narrated by whoever built it.
