## Your question: should Welcome10 work on bundles for new customers?

**Recommendation: no. Keep it off, which is how it's set today.**

| Kit price → with Welcome10 | Gross profit, no code → with Welcome10 |
|---|---|
| Tailgate $465 → $418.50 | $228.62 → $182.12 |
| Turkey 30 QT $469 → $422.10 | $219.86 → $172.96 |
| Turkey 60 QT $519 → $467.10 | $255.38 → $203.48 |
| Platinum 120 QT $892 → $802.80 | $396.00 → $306.80 |
| Ultimate 120 QT $1,001.98 → $901.78 | $440.99 → $340.79 |

- **A new customer already has a fair deal without it.** They can buy the pieces separately with Welcome10.
  - On the tailgate kit that's $436.48, and we still make $200 on it.
  - On Platinum and Ultimate, the bundle saving is already about 10%. For example, Platinum 120 QT is $892.00 as a bundle and $892.80 as pieces with the code.
- **Letting it stack gives away a second discount.** It costs $47–$100 of gross profit per bundle. On your
  per-order method, every kit goes below zero: tailgate −$18.69, turkey 30 QT −$28.71.
- **One line on each kit page** will say the bundle price already includes the discount and welcome codes
  work on everything else. That way nobody feels tricked at checkout.
