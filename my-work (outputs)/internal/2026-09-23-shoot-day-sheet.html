Tell Garrett at setup

Never on camera

  • One claim per pot: 30 QT "350° in under 10 min" · 18 QT "350° in under 5 min" · 60 QT boil "boils in under 7 min"
  • Nobody says "Made in USA" or "patent-pending." "Built in Louisiana" is fine.
  • No crawfish near a fryer · no team/school logos · no beer, soda or other-brand labels · no warranty talk

How to shoot your clips

  • Phone vertical (9:16) unless it says photo. Hold still 3 seconds before and after.
  • Sound on. Stand beside Garrett's camera, not in front of it.
  • Post means a scheduled post is waiting on it.
