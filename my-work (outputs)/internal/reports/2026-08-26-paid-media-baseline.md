## ⭐ THE HEADLINE: both channels are profitable and both are being starved

Break-even ROAS is **~2.4** (44.5% gross margin — see `2026-08-26-shopify-baseline.md`).

| Channel | Window | Spend | Platform-claimed value | Platform ROAS | vs. break-even |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Meta** | Jul 12 – Aug 24 | **$10,614** | $74,778 | **~7.0** | **2.9× over** |
| **Google** | Jul 20 – Aug 20 | **$17,436** | $93,551 | **5.37** | **2.2× over** |

**Google's own interface says it out loud:** *"You missed conversion value because you're limited by
budget."* **Three of five Google campaigns are flagged `Limited by budget`** — including both
Performance Max campaigns, which together spend $15,855 and return 4.7–5.9×.

And Meta spend has been **cut 62%** since the vendor handover (below).

**This is the +$500K.** It does not require a new channel, a new product, or a new campaign. It
requires raising budgets on two channels already clearing break-even by 2–3×.

## 3. The honest blended number

**Platform-reported ROAS double-counts.** Meta claims $74,778 and Google claims $93,551 for
overlapping windows — together roughly equal to *all* of HPC's revenue in the period. Both platforms
are claiming the same orders. **Never add them, and never report them to Jay as revenue.**

**Use blended MER instead.** August 2026 (approximate):

| | |
|---|---|
| Net sales (Shopify, Aug 1–25) | **$87,938** |
| Estimated paid spend (Meta ~$7K + Google ~$16K) | **~$23,000** |
| **Blended MER** | **~3.8** |
| Gross profit at 44.5% | ~$39,100 |
| Less ad spend | −$23,000 |
| **Contribution before overhead** | **~+$16,100** |

**Break-even MER is ~2.25. HPC is running ~3.8.** Profitable, with real headroom.

## 4. Recommendation — scale, carefully and measurably

**The single highest-value action available: raise budgets on what's already working.**

| Priority | Action | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Lift the two Google PMAX budgets** ($300 & $200/day) | Both flagged `Limited by budget` at 4.72–5.91 ROAS. Google is refusing money it says it can spend profitably. |
| 2 | **Raise Standard Shop \| Overflow off $10/day** | 8.95 ROAS — best in the account, smallest budget in the account. |
| 3 | **Rebuild Meta spend toward the prior ~$20K/mo** | ~7 ROAS held through the cut. Step up ~20–30% per week, never more. |
| 4 | **Raise DSA \| Search off $35/day** | 3.87 ROAS, budget-limited. |

**Rules for scaling**
- **Never more than 20–30% per campaign per week.** Faster resets learning and costs more than it earns.
- **Watch blended MER, not platform ROAS.** Kill the increase if blended MER drops below **2.8**
  (a 20% buffer over the 2.25 break-even).
- **Expect efficiency to decay as spend rises.** ROAS at $40K/mo will not equal ROAS at $24K/mo. The
  question is only whether it stays above break-even.
- **Capacity is not a constraint** — Evan confirmed HPC can fill a spike and will flag it if not.

⚠️ **Every one of these is a budget change. Budget changes are Evan's click, not ROUX's.**
ROUX has made no changes to either account.
