processing, break-even lands around **2.4**.

## 4. Our product mix may not match how the feed is weighted

Trailing 12 months, by net sales:

| # | Product | Net sales |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 120 QT Powered Cooker | $492,671 |
| 2 | **18 QT Fish Fryer / Brazier Powered Pot** | **$293,194** |
| 3 | Boil Boss Triple Jet Burner | $211,351 |
| 4 | 100 QT Powered Cooker | $165,835 |
| 5 | 60 Gallon Commercial Cooker | $163,772 |

**The 18 QT fryer is our #2 product for the year — and our #1 product in August**, roughly double the
120 QT in the off-season.

**Context on where we're headed:** our goal this year is to grow the **June–October window**, which
has historically been slow. The fryer line, the 4-Way Pasta Cooker, the 40 QT Sauce Cooker, and
banjo-burner setups are all year-round products. If the feed or campaign structure currently skews
toward crawfish-season SKUs, we'd like to rebalance toward those for the off-season.

**Please don't put spend behind:** steamer units, Navimow mowers, Predator grills, or Rugged Road
coolers. Those are either third-party-operated or deliberately not marketed.

## 5. Our **Scratch & Dent** line carries full margin (48.7%)

discounting the main line.** Possibly worth its own Shopping treatment.

**Organic search is our single largest discovery channel, and it beats Google Ads 5:1 on first touch.**
That's your work showing up, and it's a bigger deal than we realised.

Two directions we'd be interested in your view on:

**a) Seasonal how-to content.** Search demand in our category spikes hard on a calendar:
- Jan–May: *how to boil crawfish · how long to boil crawfish · how much crawfish per person · crawfish boil seasoning recipe · best crawfish boiler*
- Feb–Apr (Lent): *fish fry batter · how to fry fish outside*

**b) Year-round / off-season terms** — this is where our growth target lives:
- *outdoor pasta cooker · sauce cooker · banjo burner · low country boil pot · shrimp boil pot · crab steamer pot · peanut boiler · tailgate fryer*

## 8. One more note on positioning

**80% of our customers say they chose us for product quality. Only 10% mention price.** Price is our
loudest objection but almost never the reason someone buys.

So on ad copy and landing pages: **leading with quality, durability, and proof works better for us
than leading with price or discounts.** Our warranty is 5-year residential / 2-year commercial, and
performance claims need qualifiers — "up to 75% less propane," "rolling boil in as little as 6.5–7
minutes" — never flat guarantees.
