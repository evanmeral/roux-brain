# Comment & DM replies — Labor Day week
**Built 2026-09-02.** The original pack said "reply to comments, the answer is the Yeti frame" and
left it at that. This is the actual bank. Paste and adjust.

**House rules for every reply**
- Answer in one or two lines. A wall of text under a post reads as defensive.
- Never defend the price. Reframe it. Never discount in a comment beyond the codes already live.
- Never name a competitor. "Thin pots warp" beats naming anyone.
- Always "up to," "as little as," "in as fast as." Never a flat guarantee.
- If you don't know a number, say so and offer to check. Don't estimate in public.
- Move anything that needs an order number, an address or a price quote into DM.

---

## 1. "That's too expensive" / "$700 for a pot?!"

This is the #1 objection and almost never why anyone actually buys — 80% of customers cite quality,
10% cite price. Reframe from *size of purchase* to *frequency of purchase*.

**Short (comment):**
```
Fair. Think of it like a Yeti versus a foam ice chest. The foam one works — you'll just buy another one next year. With decent care these pots last generations.
```

**Longer (if they engage):**
```
Two things make it cost less over time. You buy it once instead of every couple of seasons, and the tunnel tubes mean you burn up to 75% less propane every single boil. That second one adds up fast if you cook a lot.
```

⚠️ Never claim a specific lifespan. "Lasts generations" is the register. Don't say "50 years."

## 2. "Does the 7-minute thing actually work?"

People arrive skeptical of this and are relieved when it's true — two separate reviewers volunteered
"it worked like advertised." Lean into demonstration, not insistence.

```
It does. Rolling boil in about 6.5 to 7 minutes, and back to a boil roughly 90 seconds after the next sack goes in. Put a timer on it when yours shows up — that's what most people do.
```

## 3. "Will this work on the burner I already have?"

The best answer in the whole bank, because it turns a lost sale into a cheaper one.

```
Yes — that's what our Performance pots are for. The speed comes from the tunnel tubes welded on the bottom of the pot, not the burner, so it'll run on what you've got. Cheaper way in, and you can always add our Triple Jet later.
```

## 4. "How is it faster? Is it the burner?"

Get this right every time. **The technology is in the pot.**

```
It's the pot, not the burner. We hand-weld tunnel tubes across the bottom so the burner's heat spreads over the whole base instead of hammering one spot. The pot absorbs a lot more of the energy, so it climbs faster. Patented — No. 11,844,459.
```

## 6. "Can I get it before the weekend?"

```
Most orders ship in 1–2 business days. Order today and you're in good shape — drop us a DM with your zip and we'll give you a straight answer on transit time.
```

⚠️ Never promise a delivery date in a public comment. Ship time is ours; transit time isn't.

## 7. "What's the warranty?"

The one claim most likely to get shortened into something wrong.

```
Full 2-year on everything — parts and labor, normal use. On residential pots 120 QT and under there's also a limited 5-year on the tunnel tube pot bottoms, the powered stands and all the welds. On that one the owner covers labor and shipping both ways.
```

⛔ Never write "5-year warranty" on its own. Both qualifiers, every time: **residential**, and
**120 QT or smaller**. Commercial units carry the 2-year only.

# What NOT to say

| Don't | Because |
|---|---|
| "Hard boil" | Banned. **Rolling boil** or **raging boil.** |
| Anything pairing a fryer with crawfish | A fryer is for fish, soft-shell crab, beignets, hushpuppies, fries, wings, okra. |
| "5-year warranty" unqualified | It's limited, residential, 120 QT and under. |
| A competitor's name | Ever. |
| An extra discount to close a commenter | The two live codes are the offer. |
| A performance number you don't have | "I don't have that number, let me check" is a fine public answer. |
| A guaranteed delivery date | Ship time is ours, transit time isn't. |
