# ⚠️ First, the structural point: build 4 ads, not 10

You have 10 image files. **Do not make 10 ads.** In Meta, one ad holds all three aspect ratios via
**Edit placements → Customize by placement**. Splitting them into separate ads fragments your
conversion data across 10 learning pools and none of them will ever exit learning phase.

**Four ads. Each one carrying its sizes.**

| Ad | Files | Campaign | Live |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A — Vintage** | vintage 4×5 · 1×1 · 9×16 | `BPM_TOF_Manual` | Sept 1–8 |
| **B — HPC Dark** | hpc-dark 4×5 · 1×1 · 9×16 | `18qt-TOF-Prospecting` | Sept 1–8 |
| **C — Multi-product** | vintage-multi 4×5 | New retargeting campaign | Sept 1–8 |
| **D — Final Days** | final 4×5 · 1×1 · 9×16 | Both TOF campaigns | **Sept 5–8 only** |

Placement mapping inside each ad:
- **4:5 (1080×1350)** → Facebook Feed, Instagram Feed
- **1:1 (1080×1080)** → Marketplace, Explore, right column, anywhere square
- **9:16 (1080×1920)** → Instagram Stories, Facebook Stories, Reels

# ⚠️ Two things to check before launch

**1. Do the codes apply to Scratch & Dent and already-discounted items?**
If `LABORDAY10-26` stacks on the ~30%-off Scratch & Dent line, margin on those goes thin fast.
Worth confirming the discount's product exclusions in Shopify.

**2. Free-shipping threshold, if you have one.**
10% off can drop an order under the threshold, so the customer sees shipping appear at checkout right
after they applied a discount. Bad moment. Check whether the threshold is calculated pre- or
post-discount.
