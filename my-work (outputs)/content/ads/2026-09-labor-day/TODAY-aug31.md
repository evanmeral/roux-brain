## 💰 Budget — ROUX's recommendation

**Raise `BPM_TOF_Manual` +25%. Hold `18qt-TOF-Prospecting` flat. Add retargeting.**

| Campaign | Now | Today | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| `BPM_TOF_Manual` (3 active ad sets) | $164/day | **$205/day** | $56.00 CPP, 7.60 ROAS — the efficient one |
| `18qt-TOF-Prospecting` | $50/day | **$50/day — no change** | $72.24 CPP vs a ~$63 ceiling. It's already over. |
| **New retargeting** | — | **$60–80/day** | Cheapest CAC in any account, and none exists |

**Why raise BPM when frequency is 5.20 (saturated)?** Because four new creatives land at the same
time. Raising budget on *stale* creative into a saturated audience is how you burn money; raising it
alongside fresh creative is how you use the audience you've already paid to reach. The two decisions
are only sensible together.

**Why not touch the 18 QT campaign?** It's running $72.24 per purchase against a ceiling of roughly
$63. More budget at a losing rate just loses faster. It still gets the new creative — better creative
is what could bring that cost down.

**Retargeting caveat, stated honestly:** a new campaign starts in Meta's learning phase and an 8-day
promo won't be long enough to exit it. Retargeting pools are small and warm so it usually performs
anyway, but expect noisy numbers and don't judge it on this window alone.

### Stop rule
| Signal | Action |
|---|---|
| Blended MER below **2.8** for 2 days | Roll the BPM increase back |
| BPM cost per purchase above **$70** | Roll back |
| Retargeting frequency above **3.5** | Cut its budget |

**Break-even during the promo is ~2.97, not 2.4** — the 10% discount cuts combo margin from 44.5% to
33.7%.

⚠️ **All CAC ceilings remain provisional** until Jay's Excel lands (landed BOM + overhead). Today's
increase is deliberately modest for that reason.
