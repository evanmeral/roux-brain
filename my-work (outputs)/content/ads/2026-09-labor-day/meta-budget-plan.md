# ⏰ The headline: start the ramp TODAY, not on launch day

**Today and Monday are the only working days before the sale opens.**
Aug 28 (Fri) → weekend → Aug 31 (Mon) → **Sept 1 launch.**

**Why this matters more than the budget number:** Meta resets an ad set into *learning phase* when
you raise its budget by more than ~20–30%. Learning takes 3–7 days and delivery is worse throughout.

**If you raise budgets on Sept 1, the entire 8-day promo runs inside learning phase.** You'd pay
elevated CPAs for the exact days the offer is live — the worst possible timing.

**So: ramp in steps starting today, so budgets are stable and out of learning before the offer goes live.**

## 5. Guardrails — the numbers that mean stop

| Watch | Threshold | Action |
|---|---|---|
| **Blended MER** (total revenue ÷ total ad spend) | **below 2.8** | Stop raising. Hold budgets. |
| Blended MER | below **2.4** for 2 days | Roll back one step |
| Cost per purchase, combo orders | above **$114** | Stop raising that campaign |
| Cost per purchase, fryer-led | above **$102** | Stop raising that campaign |
| Frequency (retargeting) | above **3.5** | Cut budget or widen the audience |

**Break-even ROAS during the promo is 2.97** — 10% off drops the combo's margin from 44.5% to 33.7%.
It is *not* the usual 2.25. Anything that looks fine at 2.5 is losing money this week.

**Check once a day, not hourly.** Daily numbers swing wildly at this spend level; two consecutive
days of a bad signal is a trend, one day is noise.

## 6. What this is worth

Honest framing, because platform ROAS over-claims and I won't pretend otherwise.

**The 8-day promo itself is the smaller prize.** Roughly $1,300–2,000 of extra spend at a degraded
promo margin returns maybe a few hundred to a couple of thousand in contribution. Worth doing, not
transformative.

**The real prize is what the ramp proves.** If Meta holds efficiency at $500–600/day, you've
established that the channel absorbs 2.5× its current spend profitably — and that's the finding that
funds the rest of the June–October push and, ultimately, the $500K gap.

**A promo window is the cheapest time to run that test**, because conversion rate is elevated by the
discount. If efficiency holds now, it tells you a lot. If it collapses now, it would have collapsed
worse without a discount.
