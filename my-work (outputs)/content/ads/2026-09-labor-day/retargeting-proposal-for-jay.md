# Proposal: Labor Day Retargeting Campaign
**For Jay's approval · Prepared 2026-08-31 · Requested by Evan**

---

## The ask
**$25/day for 8 days = ~$200 total.** One new Meta campaign, Sept 1–8, running alongside the
Labor Day sale.

## What it is
A campaign that advertises **only to people who have already been to our website and didn't buy.**

## Why we don't have one
Every Meta campaign we run today is **cold prospecting** — strangers who've never heard of us.
Nobody is being shown ads who already looked at a cooker and walked away. In most stores that group
is the cheapest and most profitable audience there is: they already wanted the product, and now
there's a discount and a deadline.

## Why now
1. **The sale gives them a reason to come back.** 10% off sitewide, 30% off leg extensions, expires
   Sept 8. That's exactly the nudge a warm visitor needs.
2. **The audience already exists.** IntentWave installed an identity pixel on the site and has
   already synced the list into Meta — about **4,000 identified contacts**. No setup cost, no
   waiting. It's sitting there unused.
3. **The creative is already made.** The multi-product Labor Day ad was built for exactly this
   audience — people who know us and need to pick, not be introduced.

## Why the budget is small
The audience is small, so the budget has to match it. Spending too much against 4,000 people just
shows the same person the same ad over and over:

| Budget | Times each person sees the ad over 8 days |
|---|---|
| **$20/day** | **~3× ✅ healthy** |
| $40/day | ~6× ⚠️ |
| $60/day | ~9× ❌ wasteful |
| $80/day | ~12× ❌ |

**$25/day is sized to the audience, not to ambition.**

> If the pool turns out larger than 4,000 once we check it in Meta, the budget can scale with it —
> roughly $20/day per 4,000 people. **Evan to confirm actual size before launch.**

## What we expect
Honest framing: **$200 is a test, not a growth plan.** What we're buying is an answer to a question
we can't currently answer — *does retargeting work for HPC?*

- Retargeting typically returns **2–4× the ROAS of cold prospecting**, at a much lower cost per sale
- Our cold campaigns currently run **$56–72 per purchase**
- If retargeting lands anywhere near **$25–35 per purchase**, that changes how we spend all year

## The risks, stated plainly
1. **It won't have time to optimise.** Meta needs roughly 50 purchases in 7 days to learn an
   audience. An 8-day campaign on a small pool won't get there. Warm audiences usually perform
   anyway, but the numbers will be noisy. **Don't judge the channel on this window alone.**
2. **The pool may be smaller than it looks.** 4,000 "identified contacts" is a lookalike seed;
   the true retargetable pool needs confirming.
3. **$200 is the whole downside.** That's the entire exposure.

## Stop rule
| Signal | Action |
|---|---|
| Frequency above **3.5** | Cut the budget |
| Cost per purchase above **$60** after 4 days | Pause |
| Anything under **$40** per purchase | ✅ Keep it running past the sale |

## What it does NOT change
- No increase to the two existing campaigns is being requested here — that's a separate decision
- Nothing published without Evan's approval
- **All CAC ceilings remain provisional** until the landed-BOM and overhead figures land. This budget
  is deliberately small for that reason.
