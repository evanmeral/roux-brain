# Daily monitoring routine
**Once a day, mid-morning. Not hourly — daily swings at this spend level are noise.**

Pull five numbers:
1. **Yesterday's Shopify net sales**
2. **Yesterday's Meta spend**
3. **Blended MER** = net sales ÷ total ad spend
4. **Cost per purchase** by campaign
5. **Redemptions per code** (Shopify → Discounts)

### Decision tree
| Signal | Action |
|---|---|
| MER **≥ 3.2** and CPP under ceiling | ✅ Take the next ramp step |
| MER **2.8 – 3.2** | Hold. Don't raise, don't cut. |
| MER **2.4 – 2.8** for 2 days | ⚠️ Roll back one step |
| MER **below 2.4** for 2 days | 🛑 Roll back to Aug 28 levels and tell Beau |
| CPP over **$114** (combo) or **$102** (fryer) | 🛑 Stop raising that campaign |
| Retargeting frequency over **3.5** | Cut its budget or widen the audience |

**Break-even during the promo is 2.97, not 2.25.** The 10% discount cuts combo margin from 44.5% to
33.7%. Anything that looks acceptable at 2.5 is losing money this week.

**One bad day is noise. Two consecutive days is a trend.**
