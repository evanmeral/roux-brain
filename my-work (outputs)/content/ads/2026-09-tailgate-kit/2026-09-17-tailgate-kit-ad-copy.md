### Direction A: "The speed is in the pot."
- **Idea:** teach the difference. The pot is the technology and the Triple Jet is the right burner under it. It speaks to people who looked but didn't buy, still unsure why this pot is worth more.
- **Visual:** `updated-2026-09/120qt on Boilboss TJB - lid off, hose and regulator.png` centered, on the same navy system as the kit ad. A callout arrow points at the pot bottom: "Tunnel Tubes, hand-welded here." The Triple Jet is labeled plainly.
- **Headline:** "The speed is in the pot."
- **Sub:** "Tunnel Tubes on the bottom. Triple Jet underneath. Rolling boil in as fast as 7 minutes."
- **Proof line (30px):** "Up to 75% less propane · Patent No. 11,844,459"
- **Warranty, optional:** "5-YR RESIDENTIAL WARRANTY" is allowed here, because the frame shows a single 120 QT pot (the SAFETY.md exception). If a second pot or the 160 QT appears, drop it.
- **Price:** none. It teaches rather than sells.
- **Risk:** the art has to show the tubes. The cutout is a side view, so the callout does the work. Garrett's Triple Jet B-roll ask (Sept 22) could give us a better frame.

### Direction B: "Buy it once." (Platinum bundle offer)
- **Idea:** the answer to the price objection, in two parts. It costs less to own (buy it once, the nice-ice-chest idea without naming the brand), and it costs less to run (up to 75% less propane). It closes with the packaged pair: the **Platinum Boiling Bundle** (Performance pot + Triple Jet + electronic ignition, saves $100).
- **Visual:** the Performance pot cutout (`100-120qt - perf - lid off, basket beside`) and the Triple Jet (`Boilboss TJB - with centering brackets, three-quarter`) side by side, with a "+" between. Both centered as a pair, measured together.
- **Headline:** "Buy the pot once."
- **Sub:** "Performance pot + Boil Boss Triple Jet. Up to 75% less propane, every boil."
- **Offer line:** "Platinum Boiling Bundle · from $737 · save $100". The price and saving are from `what-we-sell.md` (Finn's Shopify pull, 2026-09-10). Re-check Shopify before this runs.
- **Fitment line, if used:** "Triple Jet pairs with 60 QT and up." Nothing wider.
- **Warranty:** none. Two products in frame means both qualifiers, which the layout can't fit.
- **Risk:** the Platinum SEO title is item 3 on the Group A sheet. Fix it before this points there.
