## Product A: Tailgate Fry Kit (18 QT)

### Features & Benefits

- Tunnel Tubes hand-welded on the pot bottom: they spread the burner's heat across the whole base, so the oil hits 350° in as little as 5 minutes.
- Up to 75% less propane: the pot absorbs more of the burner's heat instead of wasting it, so one tank goes further.
- Leg extensions, about 41 inches: you fry standing up, not bent over hot oil on the pavement.
- Powered pot, 4mm aluminum, hand-welded in Louisiana: the burner is welded on and the basket is in the box. Nothing to bolt together in the parking lot.
- 5-inch fry thermometer, the one that fits this pot: you know the oil is at temp before the first basket goes in.
- 20-inch skimmer: pull the stray bits out between batches so the oil stays clean for the next one.

### Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I use a discount code on the kit?**
No. The kit price already includes the saving, so codes don't apply to it. Military and first-responder discounts still do, and welcome codes still work on everything else in your cart.

**What burner comes with it?**
It's a Powered pot, so the burner is welded to the pot. That's what Powered means. There's nothing to bolt on.

**Why the 5-inch thermometer, and no Wind Shield?**
Both are fitment calls. The 5-inch is the thermometer that fits the 18 QT; the 12-inch doesn't sit well in it. The Wind Shield is built for a pot sitting on the ground, and this rig stands on leg extensions.

**Can I put the leg extensions on a different pot?**
Only three products take them: the 18 QT Fish Fryer, the 4-Way Fryer / Pasta Cooker and the 40 QT. They don't fit anything else in the lineup.

**How fast does the oil heat up?**
The 18 QT hits 350° in as little as 5 minutes. The speed is the Tunnel Tubes on the bottom of the pot, not the burner, and they're the reason it burns up to 75% less propane. Fish, fries, wings, okra, hushpuppies.

**What's the warranty?**
Full 2-year warranty on everything, parts and labor under normal use. The pot also carries our limited 5-year warranty on residential pots 120 QT or smaller: it covers the Tunnel Tube pot bottom, the powered stand and all welds, and you cover labor and shipping both ways.

## Product B: Turkey Fry Kit (30 QT and 60 QT Two-Bird, one page)

### Features & Benefits

- Tunnel Tubes hand-welded on the pot bottom: they spread the burner's heat across the whole base, so the oil hits 350° in under 10 minutes.
- Up to 75% less propane: the pot absorbs more of the burner's heat instead of wasting it, so one tank goes further.
- One bird or two: the 30 QT fries one turkey; the 60 QT Two-Bird fries two at once, up to two 20-pounders on its dual rack.
- Burner and rack in the box, 4mm aluminum, hand-welded in Louisiana: the 6-inch banjo burner and the rack that fits the pot come with it. Nothing to hunt down Thanksgiving morning.
- Wind Shield, 12-inch fry thermometer and 20-inch skimmer: keep a November gust off the flame, know the oil is at temp before the bird goes in, and clear it after.
- Boils the rest of the year: set it up to boil and it hits a rolling boil in under 7 minutes. Shrimp, corn, potatoes.

### Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I use a discount code on the kit?**
No. The kit price already includes the saving, so codes don't apply to it. Military and first-responder discounts still do, and welcome codes still work on everything else in your cart.

**How many turkeys, and how big?**
The 30 QT fries one turkey. The 60 QT Two-Bird fries two at once, up to two 20-pounders, on the dual rack that comes with it. One table, the 30 QT. Two birds or a big crowd, the 60 QT.

**What burner comes with it?**
Both sizes come with a 6-inch banjo burner. The 30 QT is a Powered pot, so its burner is welded to the pot. The 60 QT Two-Bird ships with its 6-inch banjo burner, the dual turkey rack and the lid.

**How fast does the oil heat up?**
Both sizes hit 350° in under 10 minutes. The speed is the Tunnel Tubes on the bottom of the pot, not the burner, and they're the reason it burns up to 75% less propane.

**Can I boil in it the rest of the year?**
Yes. Set it up to boil and it hits a rolling boil in under 7 minutes. Shrimp, corn, potatoes, a low-country boil for the Fourth. One pot, all year.

**What's the warranty?**
Full 2-year warranty on everything, parts and labor under normal use. Both pots also carry our limited 5-year warranty on residential pots 120 QT or smaller: it covers the Tunnel Tube pot bottom, the powered stand and all welds, and you cover labor and shipping both ways.
