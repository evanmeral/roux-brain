High Performance Cookers builds outdoor cookers, boil pots, burners and fish fryers in Covington, about 45 minutes up the road. Made in USA by a team of about twelve who weld the pots, pack the freight and answer the phone themselves.

The speed is not in the burner. It is in the Tunnel Tubes, tubes we hand-weld across the bottom of every pot so the burner's heat spreads over the whole bottom instead of hammering one spot. Patent No. 11,844,459. That gets you a rolling boil in as fast as about 7 minutes, about 90-second recovery when the next sack goes in, and up to 75% less propane. The pots are 4mm aluminum, built to last generations with good care.

Crawfish season is on. Come by the booth, turn a pot over, and put your hands on the tubes that do it.

Feel the heat. See the speed. Taste the difference.

## Claim check

- Tunnel Tubes described as tubes hand-welded to the **bottom of the pot**; speed attributed to the pot, not the burner. ✅
- Every number qualified — "as fast as," "about," "up to." ✅
- "Rolling boil" throughout. No "hard boil." ✅
- No competitor named. ✅
- No warranty stated — a blurb this short cannot carry both required qualifiers (residential, 120 QT or smaller), so stating it at all would be a false claim. Deliberately omitted. ✅
- Fryers named as **fish fryers** and never tied to crawfish; the crawfish line refers to a pot. ✅
- Leads with what we build and where, not with price or a discount. ✅
- Patent No. 11,844,459 (issued). If a logo is uploaded, use the **shield** — the circle logo still reads "patent pending." ✅

## Before Evan submits

**1. Confirm the character cap against the actual form.** Exhibitor directory forms
almost always cap the description and rarely publish the limit in advance. Open the
form, check the field's counter or `maxlength`, then pick the version that fits.
Counts above include spaces and punctuation. If the cap is tight, the Short version
still carries the three things that matter — what we make, where we are, the 7 minutes.
