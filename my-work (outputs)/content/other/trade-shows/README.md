## 🔥 The constraint that shapes the whole booth

**Propane is not allowed in the exhibit hall.** Confirmed by Evan with the show, 2026-09-08.

**We cannot boil at this booth.** That removes the single most persuasive thing HPC can do in
front of a stranger — put a clock on a pot and let them watch it hit a rolling boil in about
seven minutes. Every booth decision follows from this:

- **The demo becomes video with a visible running clock.** Unedited, on a loop, on a screen
  people can see from the aisle. The reviews say customers arrive skeptical of the 7-minute
  claim, so the clock has to be legible, not decorative.
- **The tunnel tubes become the hands-on moment instead.** A pot turned over so people can
  touch the welded tubes on the bottom is the next-best proof, and it needs no flame. It also
  lands the fact most often mis-stated about HPC — the technology is in the pot, not the burner.
- **This raises the priority of the high-res tunnel-tube image already on order from Garrett.**
  The library's only one is 640×323. For a booth that cannot demo, that image is the demo.

## Open items on the NOLA show

Nothing is blocking until the exhibitor kit arrives. When it does, it will want:

- **Logo** — high-res PNG plus vector, transparent and white. **Shield logo, not the circle** —
  the circle still says "patent pending" and the patent has issued (No. 11,844,459).
- **Booth contact** — name, cell, email for whoever actually stands at the booth on show days,
  not necessarily whoever signed.
- **Categories** — a pick-list; it drives how attendees filter the directory.
- **Listing photos** — 2–3 lifestyle shots, and the treatment for each.
- **Show special field** — leave blank. Pricing is a Beau call, not a copy one.
- **COI and booth staff list** — usually a separate, earlier deadline than the directory.
- **Electrical order** — a screen loop needs power, and hall electrical is almost always an
  extra line item with an early-bird price that expires. Watch for it in the kit.

⚠️ **Two dates to confirm when the kit lands:** the directory copy deadline (program copy
usually closes weeks before the show, for print) and the booth staffing/COI deadline.

⚠️ **Standard 10×10, not a corner** — one open side instead of two. Sightlines and layout
have to work from a single aisle. Worth knowing before anyone designs the booth.
