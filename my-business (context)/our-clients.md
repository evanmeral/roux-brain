## The segments

### 1. Backyard Boil Host — **PRIMARY**
- **Who:** Louisiana / Gulf-Coast home cook, 30–60. Hosts 20–50 people several times a season.
- **Buys:** 80–120 QT rig, Boil Boss accessories. → The Tier 1A Performance + BB-TJB push aims here.
- **Trigger:** season opening, a birthday/graduation/holiday, or their old rig failing.
- **Objection:** *"$715 is a lot for a pot."* → Answer with propane savings math, the 5-yr warranty, and cost-per-boil over five years. Never apologize for price.
- **Where:** Meta — **Facebook skews here hard** — word of mouth, local.

### 2. Cook Team / Competition Boiler — **HIGHEST VALUE, UNDER-EXPLOITED**
- **Who:** Festival cook teams, competition crews, tailgate crews with a budget.
- **Buys:** **Multiples.** Multiple cookers, multiple burners, spares.
- **Trigger:** competition calendar, team rebuild in the off-season.
- **Why it matters:** highest AOV, repeat purchase, and they're walking billboards at events.
- **Gap:** they deserve a dedicated offer (team/bulk pricing) and a dedicated funnel. Neither exists.

### 3. Fryer Buyer — **SEASONAL SPIKE, YEAR-ROUND POTENTIAL**
- **Who:** Fish fry hosts (Lent), tailgaters (fall), turkey fryers (November).
- **Buys:** 18 QT fryer, 30 QT turkey, 60 QT dual. → The Tier 1B powered-pot push.
- **Trigger:** hard calendar dates — Lent, game days, Thanksgiving.
- **Why it matters:** the **18 QT is the #2 SKU for the year and #1 in August**. This segment is the proven answer to the year-round mandate. Best gifting SKUs in the catalog.

### 4. Commercial / Institutional — **HIGH AOV, LONG CYCLE**
- **Who:** Church fundraisers, caterers, festivals, restaurants, seafood markets.
- **Buys:** 60–140 gal boilers, Crawcuzzi, 160 QT, trailers.
- **Funnel:** lead-gen → phone/quote, not add-to-cart. **Financing matters a lot here.**
- **Handled by Stephen**, who walks buyers through freight before it's incurred.
- ~11.9% of revenue (~$342,011, 12 mo to Aug 2026, Shopify).

### 5. Homesick Transplant — **SLEEPER**
- **Who:** Louisiana natives now in Texas, Georgia, Tennessee, the Carolinas, Colorado.
- **Buys:** mid-size cookers + accessories. **Emotionally motivated.**
- **Trigger:** homesickness content, Mardi Gras/Lent, "bring the boil to your new city."
- **Why it matters:** a geo-expansion path that isn't cold. Test as a separate Meta audience.

### 6. Dealers / Wholesale — **JAY'S LANE**
Real revenue, but not a growth priority and not a marketing target. Route inbound to Jay. Over half of all HPC discounting is dealer/wholesale, not consumer promo.

### 7. BBQ / Outdoor-Cooking Creators — **A CHANNEL, NOT A CUSTOMER**
See `my-files (knowledge)/hpc-reference/affiliates-influencers.md`.

## 🔑 Why customers actually buy

**reviews.io post-purchase survey, n=20, sampled 2026-08-26:**

| Reason | % |
|---|---|
| **Product Quality** | **80%** |
| Reviews | 25% |
| Fast Shipping | 25% |
| Features | 20% |
| Customer Service | 20% |
| Friend's Recommendation | 15% |
| **Price** | **10%** |

**Price is the loudest objection and the weakest motivator.** It's the #1 thing shouted in social comments and on sales calls — *"That's way too expensive. Why would I ever buy that?"* — but only 10% of actual buyers cite it, and quality outranks it 8:1.

**What follows:**
- The people who object on price are largely **not the people who buy.** Discounting recruits the 10% while eroding margin with the 80%.
- **Never write copy that defends or apologizes for price.** Lead with quality proof until price stops being the question.
- **Evan's Yeti frame** does this well: buy once instead of repeatedly, and it costs less to run every boil. Quality economics, not a discount.
- Social proof and service (Reviews 25% + Service 20% + Friend's Rec 15%) outrank price **6:1** → prominent reviews and naming real humans beat promotions.

**NPS: 75.**

**How it works now.** On **2026-08-03** Evan personally emailed the seeded creators to tell them BM Digital was gone and that everything is in-house from here. The standing deal:

> *"if we're seeing decent content and a couple posts a month, we'll gladly send you more free product, your pick, on top of what you're earning."*

So: **free product for consistent posting, plus 5% commission on anything their link sells.** Free to join, no minimum, no exclusivity.

### ⛔ Paid content is discontinued
Evan to a creator who asked about paid work, 2026-08-04: *"sadly we will not be doing the paid content anymore, but we will reach out if we end up doing that again."*
**Do not offer paid content deals.** Offer the affiliate program and free product instead.

### The pitch that works on creators
The Aug 3 email is the template. It leads with honesty about the agency split, then: HPC started in 2020, everything handmade locally in south Louisiana, patented Tunnel Tube Technology, *"we want partners who believe that too."* Then the money. ## ⚠️ Shipping is a conversion risk, not a margin risk

**The customer pays shipping on everything.** HPC fronts it, but it's passed through — so freight is *not* a margin drag.

**It relocates the problem to checkout:**
- ~$80 on a $715 pot is an **~11% price increase revealed at the final step** — a classic cart-abandonment driver, landing right after the customer already swallowed the price objection once.
- Commercial: $200–$300 Southeast business, ~$300 Southeast residential, $250–$350 North business, **$350–$500 North residential with lift gate.** Stephen qualifies residential vs. business early, so this is handled.

⛔ **Free-shipping-in-price test was DECLINED by Jay (2026-08-26).** Consumer shipping stays as-is. Do not re-propose unprompted.
