## ⚠️ Verify every vendor's numbers

**Never take a marketing vendor's self-reported results at face value.** Reconcile every claim against Shopify revenue before repeating it to Evan, Jay, or Robert.

**Why:** HPC hired **BM Digital (now Advantage HQ)** to run influencer sourcing and Meta ads. They claimed they generated **more than three-quarters of HPC's annual revenue** while actually shipping free product to creators who never posted. It cost real money and produced no attributable sales. The claim was checkable against total revenue and nobody checked it.

**How to apply:** Judge partners on **incremental revenue**, never on activity metrics (creators contacted, product shipped, posts promised). Never ship seeded product without a signed deliverable and a deadline. Frame this as hygiene, not distrust.

**📅 2026-07-20 is the analytical dividing line.** That is the exact day BM Digital was fired (Evan, confirmed 2026-09-01 — use this date over any other). BM Digital ran both Meta and Google until then. Evan took Meta in-house; Coalition took Google. **Any data spanning that date is two different operators and must not be read as one trend.**

## How to work with Evan

- **He is the hub, and the bottleneck.** Nearly every spoke routes through him. He's full-time on marketing (~40 hrs/wk), so **the constraint is not hours, it's structure.** His words: *"everything is kind of all over the place."*
- He posts to every channel manually through native apps, one at a time, and has never used a scheduling tool.
- **The highest-value help is imposing a system and removing manual repetition** — batching, scheduling, repurposing — not finding him time.
- There is no dedicated designer, editor, or social manager.
- He wants **peer-level, opinionated, direct communication. No corporate hedging.**

## How to write for Robert and Jay

Both read the reports. Write for a **numerate co-owner**:
- Name the source of every figure.
- Separate **contribution margin** from **net profit**.
- Never present platform-reported revenue as actual revenue.
- Robert asks for **per-product economics from landed BOM**, not blended estimates.
- Expect scrutiny, and welcome it — on 2026-08-28 he corrected the CAC model and was right.
