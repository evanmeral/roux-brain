## What makes us different — the Tunnel Tube

> ⚠️ **Get this right in every ad, page, and caption. It is the single most commonly mis-stated fact about HPC.**

**Tunnel Tubes are tubes we hand-weld onto the bottom of the pot.**

The burner sits at the **center of the bottom of the pot**. Without the tubes, that heat hits one spot. The tunnel tubes **take the burner's energy and spread it across the entire bottom of the pot** — so the pot absorbs far more of the energy the burner is producing, and heats much faster.

Three things follow from that, and they matter commercially:

1. **The technology is in the POT, not the burner.** Never write copy implying the burner is what makes it fast.
2. **Because it's a pot feature, a Performance pot works on any burner the customer already owns.** That's a cheaper way into the brand than a full rig.
3. **We hand-weld every tube ourselves.** That is a real made-here story, not a marketing line. Photo: `my-skills/hpc-ad-creative/assets/lifestyle/Welder-welding-tunneltubes.png`.

## Our story

A Lafayette restaurant friend needed a better commercial seafood boiler. Todd Tunstall designed one around a simple idea: weld tubes across the bottom of the pot so the burner's heat spreads out instead of hammering one spot. The pot drinks in far more of the energy, and it gets to a rolling boil in about seven minutes — then recovers in ninety seconds when you drop the next sack in.

That's the whole company. Jay Meral built HPC around it in Covington, Robert brought the Boil Boss line in and we now manufacture it ourselves, and a team of about twelve does everything — welding the tubes by hand, packing the freight, answering the phone.

**Why the story matters commercially:** it's the moat. Bayou Classic is a big-box commodity brand. We're the local operation that actually boils. Every campaign should carry at least a trace of it.
