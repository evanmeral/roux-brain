# Content Engine

## The thesis
HPC's product is inherently cinematic — fire, steam, water, transformation, crowds. Most brands in
this category post product photos. HPC should post **moments**. The gear is the supporting actor.

## Content pillars (rotate; aim for the mix, not the order)

| Pillar | % | What it is | Job |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The Boil** | 30% | Real cooks, real crowds, steam, tables, hands | Aspiration. The lifestyle brand. |
| **The Proof** | 25% | 7-min timer, 90-sec recovery, propane comparison, 4mm thickness | Convince. Specs made visual. |
| **The How** | 20% | How to boil crawfish, seasoning ratios, purging, fry temps | Search + save + trust |
| **The People** | 15% | Customers, cook teams, creators, HPC crew, Louisiana | Community + social proof |
| **The Offer** | 10% | Promos, season urgency, new SKUs, giveaways | Direct revenue |

If the feed is more than ~20% product-on-white or promo, it's out of balance.

## Format rules
- **Vertical 9:16 first.** Reels/Shorts/TikTok are the reach engine. Everything else is a derivative.
- **Hook in the first 1.5 seconds.** Steam, fire, a timer starting, or a sack going in.
- **Sound on.** The boil *sounds* good. Use real audio, not just trending music.
- **On-screen text always** — most viewing is muted on the first pass.
- **Show the timer.** The 7-minute claim is only believable when the clock is in frame.

## 12 evergreen concepts to shoot on the next production day
1. **Timer test** — cold water to rolling boil, clock in frame, uncut
2. **Recovery test** — dump a sack, clock the return to boil
3. **Propane bake-off** — two tanks, two cooks, weigh the tanks after
4. **Sack-to-table** — one boil start to finish, hyperlapse
5. **The pour** — crawfish onto the newspaper table, slow motion
6. **4mm vs. thin** — side-by-side, tap them, show the gauge
7. **Feeds how many?** — 120 QT filling plate after plate, counting
8. **Setup in 60 seconds** — unbox to first flame
9. **Cost per boil** — on-screen math using the savings calculator
10. **Seasoning ratio** — the argument that gets comments
11. **Bluetooth ignition** — light the burner from across the yard. Pure demo magic.
12. **Cleanup** — the unglamorous thing nobody shows, which is exactly why it performs

## Repurposing chain (one shoot → many assets)
```
1 long-form YouTube (8–12 min)
  → 5–8 vertical clips (Reels / Shorts / TikTok)
    → 10+ stills for IG grid + email + ads
      → 3 ad creatives (winning clips → paid)
        → 1 blog post (YT transcript → SEO article)
          → 1 email
```
**Rule:** nothing gets shot for one platform.

## Channel adaptation
| Channel | Adaptation |
|---|---|
| Instagram | Reels primary; carousels for how-to; stories for behind-the-scenes + polls |
| Facebook | Same Reels, but longer caption with more story. FB audience reads. |
| TikTok | Native-first, faster cuts, creator-style delivery, trend-aware. **Not just cross-posts.** |
| YouTube | Long-form how-to + Shorts. Titles built for search: "How to boil crawfish" |
| LinkedIn | Commercial angle only: fundraiser volume, catering throughput, restaurant ROI |

## SEO content (YouTube + blog)
Search demand in this category is seasonal and enormous. Target:
- "how to boil crawfish" / "crawfish boil recipe" / "how much crawfish per person"
- "how long to boil crawfish" / "crawfish boil seasoning recipe"
- "best crawfish boiler" / "crawfish boiling pot"
- "how to fry a turkey" / "turkey fryer temperature" (Oct–Nov)
- "fish fry batter" / "how to fry fish outside" (Lent)
Run `marketing:seo-audit` to build the real list.
