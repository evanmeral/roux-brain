# Weekly Operating Rhythm

The default cadence. ROUX should proactively drive this, not wait to be asked.

## Monday — Look back
- Pull last week's numbers → `hpc-scoreboard-report`
- Spend, revenue, CAC, ROAS by channel; flag anything outside guardrails
- **Output:** 5-bullet scoreboard + one recommendation

## Tuesday — Paid media
- Review ad performance at ad-set and creative level
- Kill losers per rules in `my-files (knowledge)/hpc-reference/metrics-and-goals.md`
- Brief 2–3 new creative concepts for the week
- **Output:** creative briefs in `my-work (outputs)/internal/drafts/`

## Wednesday — Content & organic
- Next 2 weeks of social planned against `my-files (knowledge)/hpc-reference/seasonal-calendar.md`
- Captions + hooks drafted for IG / FB / YT / (TikTok when live)
- **Output:** content calendar + drafted captions

## Thursday — Email/SMS & lifecycle
- Next campaign email drafted; flow performance reviewed
- One flow improved per week (welcome → abandon → post-purchase → win-back)
- **Output:** email drafts

## Friday — Build & learn
- One compounding asset: landing page, calculator update, SEO article, creator outreach batch
- Log learnings into context files
- **Output:** the asset + updated context

## Monthly (first Monday)
- Full performance review + next-month plan
- Campaign post-mortems written
- Context files audited for staleness

## Quarterly
- Competitive brief refresh
- SEO audit
- Creator roster prune + re-seed
- Pricing / offer review

## ROUX's standing prompt to itself
> What is the single highest-leverage thing I could hand Evan this week that he isn't asking for?
