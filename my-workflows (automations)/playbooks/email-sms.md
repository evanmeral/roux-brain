## Priority order (do these in sequence)
**Flows before campaigns.** Flows are always-on revenue; campaigns are one-time.

## Core flows

### 1. Welcome / Pop-up (highest priority)
- **Trigger:** email capture
- **Offer:** not a discount on a $715 item — use *value*: "The Crawfish Boil Playbook" (seasoning
  ratios, lbs per person, purging, timing). A guide converts better than 10% off in this category
  and doesn't train discount behavior.
- 3–4 emails: welcome + story → the 7-minute proof → sizing guide (which QT for your crowd) → offer
- **Sizing guide is the money email.** "How many people do you feed?" → SKU recommendation.

### 2. Abandoned cart / browse abandon
- 3 emails at 1hr / 24hr / 72hr
- Objection-killing: 5-yr warranty, ShopPay financing, 1–2 day shipping, reviews
- Do not lead with a discount. Lead with the reason it's worth $715.

### 3. Post-purchase
- Order confirmation → shipping → **"Your first boil" guide** → review request (day 14) → accessory
  cross-sell (day 30)
- The first-boil guide is the highest-value email in the program: reduces returns, drives UGC,
  creates the review.

### 4. Win-back / seasonal reactivation
- **The big one for HPC:** every December/January, wake up every past customer for season.
  "Season's coming. Need a bigger pot? Accessories? A spare burner?"
- Cook teams and repeat boil hosts live here.

### 5. Accessory cross-sell
- Cooker buyers → Boil Boss line (ring, paddle, seasoning)
- Pot-only buyers → Triple Jet Burner (highest-value obvious upsell in the catalog)

### 6. Commercial lead nurture
- Separate list, separate cadence, longer, more educational
- Throughput math, financing, case studies (church fundraiser, caterer, festival)

## SMS
Use sparingly, use for time: season open, shipping cutoffs, restocks, flash windows.
Never more than ~2–4/month. Get consent properly (TCPA).
