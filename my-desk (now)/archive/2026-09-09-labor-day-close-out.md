## What this sale taught us about the account

**`18qt-TOF-Prospecting` was not a clean control.** It ran Labor Day creative (HPC Dark, 8
purchases at $22.33), so its sale-week $20.09 CPP was earned **with a discount running in it**.
The "cheap without an offer" claim is unproven and the fatigue-vs-decay test as designed
cannot run.

**`BPM_TOF_Manual` runs on video, not statics.** Six video ads were live throughout and never
stopped; the Labor Day statics were an overlay. This was only discovered on Sept 9 by reading
the account directly, and it overturned a plan to drop five new statics into it.

⏸️ **Suspended, not retracted: "$949 of $1,094 went to the treatment that lost."** It rests on
an ad-level table that credited four ads with 99.4% of campaign spend while six video ads were
demonstrably also running — almost certainly filtered to "labor" ads. **Do not repeat it until
Finn re-pulls Sept 1–8 reconciled to campaign total spend.**

## Process gaps this sale exposed

- **Seven ads shipped on a dated offer with no end date.** `Ends: Ongoing` on all of them.
  Nothing turned them off automatically. → pre-launch checklist line, Nova's queue.
- **46.7% of orders used no code.** Manual entry leaks. **Next promo uses Shopify automatic
  discounts.**
- **Two ads shared the exact name** `LaborDay_A_Vintage_Sept1-8 - Copy`, which is why the
  analysis had to call one "2nd dup." → ad naming process, Nova's queue.
- **`LaborDay_D_TunnelTube_Sept8` never delivered** — zero purchases across 30 days. The
  tunnel-tube concept has not been tested; it simply did not run.
