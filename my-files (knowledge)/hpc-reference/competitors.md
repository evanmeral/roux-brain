# Competitors & Positioning

## The field

| Competitor | What they are | Their strength | Their weakness (our wedge) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Bayou Classic** | The incumbent. Big-box distribution (Walmart, Academy, Amazon). | Ubiquity, price, brand recognition | Commodity gear, thin-gauge, no speed/fuel story, no real community |
| **Loco** | Newer, design-forward outdoor cooking | Marketing polish, retail presence | Not Louisiana-native; broad outdoor-cooking positioning, less boil-specific credibility |
| **King Cooker** | Value burner/pot brand | Price, availability | Low differentiation, thin materials, no technology story |

## Why HPC wins

| Axis | HPC | Field |
|---|---|---|
| **Speed** | Rolling boil in ~6.5–7 min, 90-sec recovery | Standard burners, no recovery claim |
| **Fuel** | Up to 75% less propane (Tunnel Tube) | No efficiency story |
| **Material** | 4mm aluminum | Thinner gauge, warps |
| **Warranty** | 2-yr full on all products; 5-yr limited on residential pots ≤120 QT | Typically 1 yr or none |
| **Origin** | Made in USA, built in Louisiana | Largely imported |
| **Technology** | **Patented** Tunnel Tube | None patented in this way |
| **Identity** | Actual Louisiana operation | National/generic |

## Positioning statement
> For south Louisiana cooks who feed a crowd, High Performance Cookers is the outdoor cooking rig
> that gets to a rolling boil in about seven minutes and does it again ninety seconds later —
> because unlike mass-market burners, HPC is built in Louisiana around patented Tunnel Tube
> technology that burns up to 75% less propane through 4mm aluminum, backed five years.

## How we compete (rules)
- **Never name them.** Compete on receipts, not on shade. "Thin pots warp" beats "Bayou Classic warps."
- **Own the time.** "Home of the 7 minute boil" is the most ownable phrase in the category. Repeat it until it's ours.
- **Own the fuel math.** The savings calculator is a genuine differentiator no competitor can copy without the tech.
- **Own the culture.** They can match a spec. They can't match being from here.
