## Burners & BTU ratings

- **Single Jet:** propane only
- **Double Jet:** included with 80 qt Powered Seafood Cooker; 0-30 PSI regulator
- **Triple Jet (Propane):** powerful for boiling, overkill for fish frying. Only standalone burner HPC sells. Screws onto triangular plate. Smaller version planned, no date
- **10" Banjo Module:** 200K BTU. Better for turkey frying (more controllable). `/products/10-banjo-burner-module-200k-btu`. Slower to boil and return than jet
- **32-Tip Multi-Jet NG Module:** 160K BTU. Circular pots (80/100/120 qt). Needs 2 lb NG service. Propane burners NOT convertible by simple swap
- **Natural Gas Triple Jet:** ~300K BTU (100K per tube). Needs 2 lb service. ~$300 including mounting hardware
- **375K BTU Burners:** rolling boil in well under 20 min (environment-dependent)
- **625K BTU 5-Jet:** 125K per jet (100-gallon commercial)
- **Dual Burner (200K BTU):** custom, commercial cookers only, not sold standalone
- **Electronic Ignition:** only on the Boil Boss burner; cannot be adapted to HPC burners. Designed to be submerged and still function. Compatible with the Boil Boss cooling ring
- **Burner clearance:** tube tops ~5–6" from pot bottom; 80/100/120 qt all sit at 5.5"
- **HPC does NOT make 6-foot standalone burners.** Large burners are pot attachments only

## Materials & construction

- **All pots and baskets: aluminum** (food-safe specific), NOT stainless
- Proprietary bottom-of-pot technology: aluminum construction allows heat transfer for fast boiling and consistent temperature during slow cooking or frying

## Valves

- **Gate valves** on larger pots (more consistent, fewer failures than ball valves)
- **Ball valves** only on the smallest fryer pot — a gate valve's rubber arm can melt from flame flare-ups on bigger pots

## Compatibility & conversion

### Natural gas
- NG burners for pots **80 qt and above** (80/100/120 qt circular; 40-gal and 60-gal commercial)
- Require **at least 2 lb pressure**
- **Do NOT come with connections or regulators** (hookups vary by site)
- **No standalone NG burners** — must attach to HPC pots
- NG→propane conversion needs assessment of the specific model; designs changed over time, so photos of the current unit are needed. Older units may not be compatible without visual confirmation
- 10" Banjo NG conversion kit and 32-Tip Multi-Jet NG conversion kit both available

### Burner interchangeability
- Burners attach to HPC pot frames, not as universal standalone units
- Attach via **3 bolts** at the corners of the triangular mounting plate
- Jet and banjo are interchangeable on the 120 qt powered seafood cooker — swap 3 screws
- **Boil Boss standalone burner CANNOT swap to the banjo option**
- Replacement: remove 3 bolts; the new burner comes on a new triangular plate

### Flame adjustment
Yellow flames = insufficient oxygen. Adjust the silver cover on the burner (where the regulator connects) to control oxygen flow and get blue flames. If the cover won't move after loosening the two screws, loosen the brass fitting on the end.

### Regulator adjustment
To increase PSI, loosen the nut toward the black handle. Fully loosening to the top opens the regulator to the full 30 PSI. The nut position can set a custom "maxed out" stop.

## Returns & refunds

- **30-day window** from receipt. Item must be unused, with tags, in original packaging. Receipt or proof of purchase required
- **12% restocking fee** for non-defective/non-damaged returns (covers return shipping and processing)
- **Customer must request the return FIRST.** Items sent back without requesting will NOT be accepted. HPC sends the label and instructions
- Customer must notify HPC when the item is dropped off
- Refund movement within 24–48 hours of initiation
- **HPC pays return shipping for defective/damaged.** Non-defective: customer pays (may substitute for the 12% fee if lower). Fees may be waived at HPC's discretion
- **Return address:** High Performance Cookers, 17991 Painters Row Suite #10, Covington, LA 70435

### Damaged / defective
- Replacement parts shipped for defective items (regulator, lid, thermometer, burner)
- Customer often keeps the damaged item as a backup
- Return labels provided for double-shipped or damaged units
- Pre-packaging checklist used before shipping
- **Damage refund examples:** ~$160 refund, 25% refund, $100–$150 credit for scratched pots
- Returns must wait until items show delivered before a label can issue
- Customer must start the carrier claim (UPS) for missing/undelivered items

### Used pot returns
- **Using a pot voids warranty for damage claims**
- Used pots returned for damage typically get a scratch-and-dent replacement, not new
- **Resale value:** cosmetic damage unused → 15% discount ($600 on a $705 pot). Used → 30% discount ($494 on a $705 pot)

### Exchanges & cancellations
- Exchange: customer ships original back → HPC inspects → customer pays difference + fees → new item ships
- Orders can be cancelled if not yet shipped

- Small items packed together (turkey rack inside the basket box; paddle inside the Boil Boss ring box)
- Estimated times are typically accurate but **not guaranteed**
