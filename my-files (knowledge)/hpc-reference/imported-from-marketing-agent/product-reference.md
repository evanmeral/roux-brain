## Pot configuration — 3 ways to buy

- **Stock Pot Only** (60–120 QT): no burner, for customers who already own one. `/products/performance-seafood-boiling-pots`
- **Pot + Separate Burner (Boil Boss Bundle)** (60–120 QT): one detachable Triple Jet burner reusable across pot sizes; ~50% less propane. `/collections/product-bundles`
- **Powered Cooker — welded burner stand** (18–120 QT): legs welded to pot; modular burner swaps jet↔banjo with 3 bolts. `/collections/powered-cookers`

Decision: owns a burner → Stock Pot · wants one burner for many sizes → Bundle · always cooks the same size → Powered Cooker

## ⛔ Burner types — SAFETY CRITICAL

- **Jet burner = BOILING** (crawfish/shrimp/crab) — high heat, fast boil
- **Banjo burner = FRYING** (turkey/fish) — controlled even heat
- **NEVER recommend a jet burner for frying** — oil overheats dangerously. Frying = banjo, always.

## Boil speed

- Residential pots (≤120 QT): boil in ~6.5–7 min; **return to full boil ~90 sec** after adding seafood (the headline benefit — prevents mushy seafood)
- Commercial 100-gal: ~10 min to boil; <5 min return
- **Don't blend residential + commercial figures**

## Cooling Ring / Cajun Halo — describe correctly

Wraps around the **OUTSIDE** of the pot; cool water circulates through the ring to cool the pot exterior and the water inside, controlling soak temp. **Never say it goes inside the pot.** Cross-sell for any boiling/crawfish customer.

## Natural gas

**The natural-gas option is only offered on 80 QT and up.** Cookers under 80 QT cannot be converted (no conversion kit) — tell the customer directly. On NG-capable units (80 QT+), standard household ½-lb service works with the 32-tip multi-jet burner; on household NG, speed ≈ a standard non-HPC pot, so propane + Tunnel Tube is fastest.

## Troubleshooting

- **Patina / dark pot:** normal & safe. Clean: ~1 tbsp white vinegar per cup water, boil until dark, drain, repeat
- **Drain valve leak:** tighten first; small leaks usually stop after first use; if persistent, remove valve, add plumber's tape, reinstall (don't cross-thread). 3/4" valve is garden-hose compatible with a 3/4" hose-to-MIP adapter
- **Vapor lock:** pot bottom overheats when unapproved objects block water circulation — only use HPC-approved accessories inside the pot
- **Cleaning:** dish soap + soft sponge; NEVER steel wool/brillo; never leave wet seasoning (salt corrodes aluminum); store basket dry/separate

## ⛔ Safety — always answer fully

OUTDOOR USE ONLY (never indoors/garage/enclosed) · Never light without water/oil in the pot · Light the flame BEFORE opening gas · Never leave a heated pot unattended · Jet burner ≠ frying · Steaming: keep 3"+ water, open lid slowly from rear · Propane tank 20"+ from burner; leak-test before use

### Clogged jets / yellow or orange-red flame — very common

A **yellow, orange or red flame** almost always means a **jet is clogged**. Jet holes are **aluminum, so they do NOT rust** — they clog from food particles, seasoning or debris falling under the pot (often when the pot is rinsed). **Fix:** clear the orifice with a paper clip, small wire or pipe cleaner and blow it out with air. If only one jet burns yellow, that's the clogged one. Burner **tubes** are **steel**, so light surface rust is normal and usually still works — replacement tubes at `/products/burner-tube-replacements`; severely rusted module → `/products/triple-jet-burner-module-375k-btu`. Only escalate if cleaning doesn't resolve it.

### Stands — what we do and don't offer

We do **not** offer individual/standalone stands **except** the triple-jet Boil Boss burner. Every other burner sold as "standalone" is interchangeable with existing HPC cookware. **Never tell a customer we'll "build a custom stand."**

### Powered cooker vs. pot + separate burner

**Powered Cooker** is the staple: legs welded to the pot bottom, burner on a triangular plate bolted to the legs, burners swap by bolt — including to a 10" banjo for slow-cook/frying, or natural gas (80 QT+). **Pot + separate Boil Boss burner** gives one detachable burner across multiple pot sizes and uses ~50% less propane. Powered = grab-and-go for one size; separate burner = flexibility across sizes.
