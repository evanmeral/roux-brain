## TikTok — the open lane
TikTok is the single largest un-worked channel. The category is *made* for it: steam, fire,
sound, transformation, crowds. Competitors are not dominant there.

**⚡ New evidence (2026-08-26): TikTok already shows up as a first-touch source at 5%, tying Instagram
— despite HPC having no account.** Customers are discovering HPC through other people's TikTok
content, at zero cost and zero effort. That is the strongest possible argument for showing up.

**Recommendation:** launch it, but do **not** treat it as another posting destination. Two low-cost moves:
1. **Recycle first.** Every Reel already produced goes to TikTok same-day. Near-zero incremental cost.
2. **Then originate.** Once there's signal, shoot native-first vertical: 7-minute-boil timers,
   sack dumps, propane-tank comparisons, "how much does a boil cost."

Ask before launching: who owns posting, and do we want @highperformancecookers reserved now?
(Handle squatting is a real risk — reserve it regardless.)
