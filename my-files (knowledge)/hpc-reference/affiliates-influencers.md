## ROUX's read on this program

**The salvage is done. The question now is what replaces it.**

Three observations that still hold:
1. **No structured onboarding existed.** Product shipped with no deliverable agreement, no deadline,
   no consequence. That is the root cause and it is a *process* fix, not a creator-selection fix.
2. **Wrong product for the creator.** 44 of 75 got the 18 QT fryer. A fryer is a nice product but it
   isn't the 7-minute-boil story. Only 4 creators got the flagship 120 QT — the demo that sells the brand.
3. **Wrong creator profile.** Heavy national BBQ/general-food skew. The audiences that buy a $715
   crawfish cooker are Louisiana/Gulf-Coast boil and outdoor accounts.

**Recommended fixes (in order):**
1. **Contract before shipment.** Simple one-pager: 1 Reel + 3 stories + affiliate link, within 30
   days of receipt, or product is invoiced. Ship nothing without a signed deliverable.
   *(This is the exact failure that cost HPC real money once already.)*
2. **Rescue the 24 active affiliates.** They already converted, they already have free gear, and Evan
   already hand-picked them as the ones who deliver. Give them a reason to post again — higher
   commission tier, early access, a codes leaderboard. **Cheapest revenue in the file, by far.**
3. **Re-approach the top 10 by reach with a paid offer — carefully.** Free product failed with them,
   and it failed under an agency with no accountability. A paid whitelisting deal (HPC runs their
   content as an ad) usually works where gifting didn't. Whitelisted creator content typically
   outperforms brand-produced ads on Meta. **Direct relationships only — no agency intermediary.**
4. **Change the seed product.** Default to the **120 QT** for anyone over 50K followers in the
   Gulf-Coast/boil niche. The demo is the product.
5. **Geo-weight the roster.** Target LA/TX/MS/AL/FL-panhandle creators, and separately, transplant
   creators ("Louisiana boy in Texas") — see segment 5 in `my-business (context)/our-clients.md`.
6. **Track it.** Every creator gets a unique discount code. No code, no seed. Then this file gets a
   revenue column and the program becomes measurable instead of hopeful.
