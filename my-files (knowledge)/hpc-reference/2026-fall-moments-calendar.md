**How to read this file**

## 3. Turkey-fry content timing

**What I found**
- Google Trends could not be read. The page returned HTTP 429 (rate-limited) on both a direct fetch and the browser. **The weekly curve for "turkey fryer" and "fried turkey" is unconfirmed.** Evan or Coalition can pull it in a logged-in browser in a minute.
- Google's own Thanksgiving search write-up: "how to deep fry a turkey" and "how long to fry a turkey" reach the top 10 searches **on Thanksgiving Day itself** ([Google blog, Nov 22 2017](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/search/lets-talk-turkey-thanksgiving-food-trends/)).
- Safety coverage clusters in **mid-November**. Examples: New Jersey Division of Fire Safety fryer guidelines, Nov 15 2017 ([nj.gov](https://nj.gov/dca/news/news/2017/approved/20171115.html)); Carrier Management / NFPA "Thanksgiving top day for cooking fires", Nov 13 2025 ([Carrier Management](https://www.carriermanagement.com/news/2025/11/13/281415.htm)); State Farm cooking-fire release covering through Nov 2025 ([State Farm newsroom](https://newsroom.statefarm.com/state-farm-releases-latest-cooking-fire-statistics-and-safety-tips-to-help-prevent-kitchen-fires/)).
- HPC's own buying pattern (internal, not outside research): 164 of 294 turkey-fryer orders in the 2025 season came Nov 1–15 (Shopify, per `seasonal-calendar.md`, 2026-09-11).

**What I conclude (not confirmed)**
- Public search interest most likely builds from **early November** (week of Nov 2–8, 2026), climbs through the week of Nov 16, and peaks Thanksgiving week. Media safety coverage follows around **Nov 12–20**.
- HPC buyers move earlier than the public search curve (first half of November). So paid turkey-fryer content should be live by **about Oct 19–26**, one to two weeks before search picks up. That matches the "launch ads by Oct 1" line already in the seasonal calendar.
- Organic how-to and safety content (thaw it, dry it, outdoors, oil level) has an open lane in **late October**, before the mid-November safety news cycle.
