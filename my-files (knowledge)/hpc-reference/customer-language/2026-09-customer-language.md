## Guardrails: read before lifting any line

- **The speed is in the pot, not the burner.** Many reviewers praise the burner ("badass burner", "sounds like a jet engine", "the burners are the best part"). Those work as sound and feel. Never use them to say the burner is what makes it fast.
- **Don't let a quote beat our own claim.** Some reviewers report times faster than we advertise: "boiling in a minute" (Derek Anderson, 2025-01-21), "4 min" (Roddy, 2022-07-13), "4.3 minutes" (Denise, 2021-04-16). Leave these out. Our line stays "about 7 minutes."
- **Slow times exist too.** Burner-module and natural-gas reviews report 12, 15 and 16 minutes, and Robert's 60 QT fryer took "35-40 min" to heat. Only quote a time next to the product it was measured on.
- **Made in USA.** Derek Anderson's 🇺🇸 emoji can read as a USA claim. Don't pair it with origin copy. A 2021 1★ (Richard) and a 2021 3★ (Matthew) both complain the product is not US-made.
- **Warranty.** No quote here states a warranty term. Don't add one next to a quote.
- **Turkey size.** Rhonda (20 lb), Robert (16.5 lb) and Bryan (18.5 lb) fried birds over USDA's 12 lb guidance. Quote only the heat-up parts.
- **Product labels in the feed are sometimes wrong.** Several reviews filed under "Boil Boss Triple Jet Burner" are about cooling the pot, and Shan's "Leg Extensions" review is about a fish fryer. Read the text, not the label, before tying a quote to a product.
- **Names.** Past HPC creative has used the reviewer's first name. Keep to first names only.
