# Seasonal Calendar

**HPC's business is a calendar business — but the mandate is to STOP it being one.**

> ⭐ **Evan, 2026-08-26:** *"Main point: don't settle for slow season, we want to grow and growth can
> only happen through selling year round."* The $3.0M → $3.5M+ target is **+$500K, and it has to come
> mostly from June–October**, because Jan–May is already worked and incremental spend into a peak
> hits diminishing returns fastest.
>
> **This is not a product problem — the year-round products already exist.** Fryers (18/30/60 QT),
> 4-Way Pasta Cooker, 40 QT Sauce Cooker, banjo burners, steamer trays as attach, Zydeco Fire
> consumables, merch, Scratch & Dent. See the priority tiers in `my-business (context)/what-we-sell.md`.

> says "outdoor cooking company that peaks at crawfish."

Dates below are the *demand* windows; campaigns must launch 3–6 weeks ahead of each.

*"we will make it work, thats a great problem to have."* Commercial is even better: the metal

Evan will flag it if marketing ever outruns the shop. → **Market aggressively; don't self-limit.**

## The two revenue peaks
1. **Feb–May (crawfish)** — the business. Most of the year's revenue.
2. **Nov (turkey + BFCM)** — the second peak, materially smaller but high-margin on accessories.

## The valley — where the +$500K lives
**June–October is the target, not a write-off.** Historically soft, but it is where the growth has to
come from. Two jobs run in parallel:

**1. Sell into it (primary).** Fryer line, 4-Way Pasta Cooker, 40 QT Sauce Cooker, banjo burners,
steamer-tray attach, Zydeco Fire consumables, merch, Scratch & Dent entry pricing. Reposition the
brand around *"whatever's in season"* rather than crawfish alone.

**2. Build during it (secondary).** Shoot next season's content while the weather's good, seed
creators so their posts land in January, do site/CRO work.

These are not sequential. The old "August is a build month" framing is **retired** — it conceded the
quarter, which is exactly what Evan rejected.
